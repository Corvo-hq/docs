---
title: "Discord"
description: "Bind a channel for investigation alerts and follow-up threads."
---

An admin can connect one Discord alert channel to a workspace.

1. Open **Integrations** and find **Discord**.
2. Invite the Corvo bot to your Discord server.
3. Select a server and a channel where the bot can post.
4. Save the channel and check for **CHANNEL BOUND**.

Corvo posts investigation alerts to the bound channel. Reply to an alert to start its investigation thread; later replies stay with that thread. In a bound channel, mention the bot when starting a separate conversation. The `/stop` and `/steer` commands can stop or redirect an active agent turn.

If a server or channel is missing from the picker, check the bot's Discord permissions and refresh the list. Use **Disconnect** on the integration card to remove the channel binding.
