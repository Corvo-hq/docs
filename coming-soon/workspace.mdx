# Work together in Corvo

Workspace is planned for teams that want to manage Corvo together. It will bring members, integrations, and shared settings into one place.

This feature is coming soon. The capabilities below describe the current plan and are not available as a Workspace feature yet.

## Invite your team

Invite members to a Workspace and assign an Admin, Editor, or Viewer role. Detailed permissions will be documented when the roles are finalized.

## Share integrations

The GitHub App connection will belong to the Workspace. Members will work with the integrations configured for that Workspace.

A GCP project or other data source will be connected to one Workspace at a time. The same data source will not be shared across multiple Workspaces.

## Keep Telegram in your workflow

Telegram direct messages and group chats will remain part of the workflow. Workspace settings are planned to show which account is connected for direct messages.
