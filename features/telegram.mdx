---
title: "Telegram conversations"
description: "Ask Corvo questions and receive investigation updates."
---

After [connecting the chat bot](/integrations/telegram), send a message in the bound DM or group. Include the workload, time range, and symptom when asking about an error. Corvo's answers and actions depend on the connected data sources, repositories, tools, and permissions.

The separate alerts bot sends investigation posts to its connected destination. Follow the investigation link to inspect grouped alerts, findings, and any proposed PR.

Send `/daily_report` in the chat to request a recent error summary. `/stop` stops an active agent turn; `/steer` lets you redirect it. See [Daily reports](/features/daily-reports) for scheduled delivery.
