---
title: "Daily reports"
description: "Review the previous 24 hours of errors and their handling."
---

A daily report summarizes the previous 24 hours of error activity. It groups related errors, shows occurrence counts and handling status, and includes available Cloud Logging or PR links.

## Schedule delivery

Open **Settings → Daily report** in your workspace. Choose a **Timezone** and **Push time** for Telegram delivery. Leave Push time unset if you only want reports on demand. The workspace's timezone determines the local calendar day used in report history.

## Request and read a report

Send `/daily_report` to the connected Telegram chat bot for an on-demand report. If generation is already running or a cooldown applies, follow the bot's response before asking again.

Open **Daily reports** in Corvo. **Report history** lists the newest reports first, one per local calendar day. Open a report to read its time range, summary, and error groups. Follow a group's **Pull request** or **Error log** link when available. A **PR OPEN** label means the proposed fix still needs review.
